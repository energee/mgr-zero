Vocabulary — decided 2026-09-02, supersedes earlier wording in this file: a brand is the sellable identity (“Lupula”, “Waves”) and is what SKUs, price tiers and every published menu hang off — it replaces the word product throughout, because a batch is a production instance (“Lupula 3”) and the two are not the same thing. Identity is optional at brew and required at packaging: a batch carries an intended brand at most, and lots.brand_id is where it becomes binding — so two batches can blend and ship as one new brand. A format is the sellable shape (glass, four-pack, case, sixtel, half) and carries bbl_per_unit; formats are packaged (their own stock) or poured (drawn from a packaged unit, never held), which is what a Square variation actually is. A SKU is brand × packaged format. Bins are physical subdivisions of a location and menus read them, so availability is derived from stock rather than hand-flipped. Sale channels become configurable rows, not an enum, and a price tier is scoped to one. Where a frame below still prices or names a serving directly, the frame is older than this note and the note wins. Open: whether formats are fully sized or shape-only; whether tiers price by format alone or brand × format; and whether a poured format binds to one packaged format or to a brand.
