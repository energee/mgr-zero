---
title: "Catalog & pricing"
description: "Brands, formats, SKUs, sale channels, price groups and prices."
---

A brand is the beer's sellable identity — name, style, ABV. A
format is the physical package (keg, can, bottle); it owns `unitsPerCase` and
`bblPerUnit`. A poured format (a pint, a taster) never holds stock: it is a
ratio back to the keg it pours from, belongs to a price group rather than a
brand, and never becomes a SKU. A SKU is one brand × one packaged format,
with an optional UPC. A keg SKU may also name a keg pool (`kegPoolId`, read
from `list_keg_pools`), whose per-keg deposit wholesale orders charge. The pool
must be in service and the format a keg with a keg size, or the request fails;
the pool's kind sets the SKU's container source, so you never send one. A SKU
already linked to a pool that is later taken out of service keeps the link.

`update_sku.batchShare` is the default fraction of a batch's planned beer
volume packaged into that SKU, greater than 0 and at most 1. Omit it to keep
the share; send `null` to clear it. All SKU shares for a brand, including
inactive SKUs, total at most 1. Only active SKUs with a share project batch
packaging demand. New SKUs start without a share; configure one with
`update_sku` after creation. The brewery's `days_to_package` starts at 14;
`set_brewery_operating_defaults.daysToPackage` accepts whole days from 1 to
120 and omission preserves the existing estimate.

Prices live on a grid, not on SKUs. A price group is a row of that grid and a
brand sits on one; it also owns the poured formats that become columns on the
grid, shared by every brand on it. A sale channel is the column and a
customer sits on one.
`set_channel_price` fills one cell — sale channel × price group × format —
with an integer `unitPriceCents`, and every SKU of that group and format sells
at it on that channel. `clear_channel_price` empties the cell, which unprices
those SKUs on that channel. There is no per-SKU exception.

A sale channel's name is unique within its brewery. Names are compared after
trimming whitespace and ignoring case, so `dtc` cannot be added beside `DTC`.
`upsert_sale_channel` returns `409 duplicate_channel_name` for a clash and
changes nothing.

## clear_channel_price [#clear_channel_price]

`command` · admin, sales

Empty one cell of the price grid; SKUs on that group and format become unpriced on that channel

| Field | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `saleChannelId` | uuid | yes |
| `priceGroupId` | uuid | yes |
| `formatId` | uuid | yes |

```bash

  -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -H "authorization: Bearer $MGR_TOKEN" \
  -d '{ "breweryId": "00000000-0000-0000-0000-000000000000", "name": "clear_channel_price", "requestId": "11111111-1111-1111-1111-111111111111", "input": { "saleChannelId": "00000000-0000-0000-0000-000000000000", "priceGroupId": "00000000-0000-0000-0000-000000000000", "formatId": "00000000-0000-0000-0000-000000000000" } }'
```
