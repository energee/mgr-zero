---
title: "Delivery & routes"
description: "Routes, stops, departure, delivery and return."
---

A route groups a driver's shipped orders and picked stock transfers into one
run. It is built, saved, departed, and returned. Each stop is confirmed as
closed with `confirm_delivery` along the way (delivered, partly refused, or
fully refused), and `return_route` refuses until every stop on the route has
an outcome. Refused beer stays outstanding until `check_in_refused_return`
puts it back, even after the route has returned.

An unattempted open stop leaves a departed route through `leave_delivery_for_later`. Its shipment or transfer becomes unassigned for another route without refusal, invoicing or stock movement. Only the assigned driver or an Admin may do this; a shipment also records an order event. `confirm_delivery` accepts `closed` only for compatibility with exact completed request replay. Fresh `closed` requests are refused after replay lookup; historical database rows remain valid.
