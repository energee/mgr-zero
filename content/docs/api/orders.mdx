---
title: "Orders & invoicing"
description: "Orders from draft to shipped, picks, allocations, invoices and credit memos."
---

An order moves `draft → submitted → confirmed → picked → shipped`, or
`cancelled` any time before shipping. Line items are `{ skuId, qty }` and prices
are snapshotted from the customer's price group when the order is created, so a
later price change never rewrites an existing order. Mutations return
`{ order_id }` unless noted otherwise.

`cancel_order` requires a reason containing non-whitespace text. New cancellations
trim leading and trailing whitespace before recording the reason; blank reasons
are rejected with `invalid_input`. Retry an uncertain cancellation with its exact
original input and `requestId`: committed historical requests, including older
whitespace-only reasons, replay their original result without another cancellation.

Confirming creates allocations and returns `{ order_id, warnings }`, where
`warnings` is `[{ sku_id, atp }]` for any SKU whose available-to-promise went
negative. Adjusting lines returns the same shape, listing each SKU now on the
order that is below zero. Setting a standing allocation returns
`{ allocation_id, status, warnings }`, and warns only when the set leaves its
SKU below zero; a release never warns. These are soft warnings. The write still
succeeds, because the brewery, not the API, decides whether to oversell.

Shipping is one transaction: inventory movements, allocation fulfilment and
(unless deferred) the invoice, returning `{ order_id, invoice_id }`.
`invoiceTiming: "on_delivery"` ships without invoicing yet — `invoice_id`
comes back null, and `confirm_delivery` invoices later at the shipped
quantities and order prices. A credit memo reverses
part of an invoice as negative lines at the original prices plus return-in
movements. Invoices are never edited.

`sync_qbo_payments` freezes each pushed document's Invoice or CreditMemo type and current-company identity. It reads the matching QuickBooks endpoint and applies the entire observed batch atomically. A confirmed missing credit memo becomes deleted; current state-report facts and exports then exclude its money without changing original local amounts, physical return movements, or filed snapshots. Deleted deposit refunds also stop reducing current keg-deposit balances without changing physical keg-return events. An explicit successful recreation makes the original credit money count again; filed snapshots remain immutable. Optional provider RemainingCredit is validated but is not stored as an invoice balance. Credit balances never count as invoice cash or prove payment or voiding. Uncertain or malformed provider responses leave the request unresolved for exact retry. Legacy invoice-only batches retain their exact target set on replay.

`get_order_cogs` reads one order's cost and margin. Amounts are in cents.
`cost_cents` is the frozen packaging-close cost of the lots shipped, less units
returned. `revenue_cents` is the order-line price times units shipped less
returned, so an order with `on_delivery` invoicing already has revenue. A
shipped unit with no lot, or whose lot has no cost, counts in `uncosted_qty`. While
`uncosted_qty` is above zero, or revenue is zero, `margin_pct` is `null`. An
order that is not found answers 404 `not_found`.

Order and invoice history queries page with a cursor; see [History paging](/docs/api#history-paging).

The legacy invoice `paid_at` field is a payment-confirmation/state marker, not an actual settlement date. Older values can be invoice modification times; newer confirmations use the sync transaction time. Do not display it as a payment date. Current balance and remote state still determine whether an invoice is payable. A partial payment remains due; zero balance with positive attributable cash is paid, and credit-only closure is settled. No actual settlement date is supplied for either one or multiple payments.

An invoice QuickBooks never received takes payments recorded in MGR (`record_invoice_payment`), and MGR's ledger owns its balance. For such an invoice, `paid_at` is the brewery-local start of the day its last payment was received. `list_invoices` carries the unvoided sum as `local_paid_cents`. `get_invoice` carries each payment, voided ones with their reasons, as `payments`, and the unvoided sum as `localPaidCents`.

An Admin can write off such an invoice with `write_off_invoice` before it is fully paid, for its remaining balance. Its payments stay, and it takes no new payment.

`list_invoices` with `status: "unpaid"` keeps only invoices that still owe money: not written off, not voided or deleted in QuickBooks, and either with a positive QuickBooks balance or, with no balance known, not marked paid. An invoice whose balance is zero or a credit is left out, even when it was never marked paid.

`adjust_order_lines` requires a reason containing more than whitespace. New
adjustments store the reason with surrounding whitespace removed; blank reasons
return `invalid_input` without changing lines or reservations. Retry a saved
request with its original input and request ID, including the original reason
spacing. Completed historical requests replay before this new-write validation.
