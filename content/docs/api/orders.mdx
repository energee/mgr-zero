---
title: "Orders & invoicing"
description: "Orders from draft to shipped, picks, allocations, invoices and credit memos."
---

An order moves `draft → submitted → confirmed → picked → shipped`, or
`cancelled` any time before shipping. Line items are `{ skuId, qty }` and prices
are snapshotted from the customer's price group when the order is created, so a
later price change never rewrites an existing order. Mutations return
`{ order_id }` unless noted otherwise.

Confirming creates allocations and returns `{ order_id, warnings }`, where
`warnings` is `[{ sku_id, atp }]` for any SKU whose available-to-promise went
negative. These are soft warnings. The confirm still succeeds, because the
brewery, not the API, decides whether to oversell.

Shipping is one transaction: inventory movements, allocation fulfilment and
(unless deferred) the invoice, returning `{ order_id, invoice_id }`.
`invoiceTiming: "on_delivery"` ships without invoicing yet — `invoice_id`
comes back null, and `confirm_delivery` invoices later at the shipped
quantities and order prices. A credit memo reverses
part of an invoice as negative lines at the original prices plus return-in
movements. Invoices are never edited.
