---
title: "Customer portal"
description: "The operations a customer portal user may call."
---

Callers with the `customer` role are portal users, not staff. They see only
their own customer's data, and these are the only operations that role can call.
The scope is enforced server-side, not by which operations the client chooses
to show.

A portal user sees their customer's id and name, its ship-to addresses, and the
prices for their own catalog. Customer bookkeeping, tax, license, and payment terms,
and the brewery's full price grid, are never returned to a portal user.

Portal orders are wholesale orders shipped from the brewery's warehouse, and
they move through the same lifecycle staff orders do. A portal user drafts,
updates, and submits. Confirming, picking, and shipping stay with the brewery.

Order and invoice history queries support cursor or numbered paging; see [History paging](/docs/api#history-paging).

Portal invoice results expose legacy `paid_at` only as a confirmation/state marker. Do not render it as a settlement date: older values may be invoice edit times, and new confirmations use sync time. Use current balance and remote state for payable status. Partial payments remain due, and credit-only closure is settled rather than paid. The portal supplies no actual payment date for one or multiple payments. For an invoice paid in full by payments the brewery recorded in MGR, `paid_at` is the brewery-local start of the day the last payment was received.

## get_portal_account [#get_portal_account]

`query` · customer

Portal: the caller's customer, ship-tos, this login's membership, keg deposits held, and palletRules (how the brewery stacks a pallet: maxLb, maxHeightIn, palletHeightIn and kegLayers, each a count per keg size); peer portal users are never listed

Takes no input.

```bash
curl -X POST "$MGR_URL/api/command" \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -H "authorization: Bearer $MGR_TOKEN" \
  -d '{ "breweryId": "00000000-0000-0000-0000-000000000000", "name": "get_portal_account", "input": {} }'
```

## portal_catalog [#portal_catalog]

`query` · customer

Portal: orderable SKUs with the caller's prices and their offers: badge is the packaged-stock availability (null when the SKU is not orderable from stock) and releases are the open releases, each with its ships-from and order-by dates, its own badge, fromBatch (true for a release from a planned batch, which the Pre-order page lists; false for one from packaged stock), bblPerUnit (the package's barrels), and the buyer's ceiling when one applies: limitLeftBbl (barrels of that batch still open to them, null when none or lifted) and limitLiftsOn, and lbPerBbl (pounds a barrel of that batch weighs by its latest gravity reading, null before one); bblPerUnit (the package's barrels) and packagingLb (pounds the package adds to the beer) give a unit's weight, and heightIn is how tall one keg stands or one case sits in a layer (null when not set); packageType, kegSize, unitsPerCase and palletFit (advisory: how many fill one pallet unit) describe its package; product is the brand, brandAlias its alias (null when none) and formatName the format, which name the line. A SKU with no open offer is left out

Takes no input.

```bash
curl -X POST "$MGR_URL/api/command" \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -H "authorization: Bearer $MGR_TOKEN" \
  -d '{ "breweryId": "00000000-0000-0000-0000-000000000000", "name": "portal_catalog", "input": {} }'
```
