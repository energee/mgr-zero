---
title: "Integrations"
description: "What QuickBooks, Square and Slack show when MGR writes into them — drawn in each product's own design language, not MGR's."
---

These are not MGR screens. They are the external surfaces an integration
produces: an invoice as Intuit renders it, a taproom sale as Square's Dashboard
lists it, a notification as Slack delivers it. Each is drawn in that product's
own design language, because the point of drawing them is to make an
integration contract legible — what MGR writes, what it must never touch, and
what comes back.

MGR's own screens are in the [screen inventory](/docs/screens).
