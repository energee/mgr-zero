Your login starts with an invitation from the brewery. Open the email, enter **Your name**, choose a password of at least eight characters, and select the **Join** button, which shows your brewery's name. Your customer membership sends you to Shop. All invited customer users have buyer access; there is no Account admin role or public sign-up.

## Sign in and navigate [#access]

<Screen name="Portal sign in" />

Browser tabs identify **Portal sign in**, **Reset password**, and **Invitations**, followed by **· MGR**.

Opening a portal page or an invoice payment link while signed out takes you to **Sign in to your account**, the portal sign-in page. **Forgot password?** keeps you in the portal's password-reset flow. Brewery staff select **Brewery staff? Sign in to MGR** to reach the staff sign-in, which links back with **Customer? Sign in to the portal**.

Enter your **Email** and the **Password** you chose, then select **Sign in**. A customer-only account opens **Shop**, or **Invitations** first if a pending invitation is waiting for you; see below. **Forgot password?** opens **Reset your portal password** with an empty email field and no sent confirmation. Enter your email and select **Send reset link**. Only after the request succeeds does the page say **If that email is on an account, a reset link is on its way.** This does not reveal whether an account exists. If the request fails, the page asks you to try again instead of claiming a link was sent. **Back to sign in** returns to the portal sign-in page. The link opens **Set new password**. A reset link that was used or has timed out opens **Reset link expired**; select **Request a new link** to go back to the email form.

While sign-in is being checked, the button says **Signing in…** and cannot be submitted again. Wrong credentials show **Incorrect email or password.** here in the portal, keeping your email in the form and leaving the password blank. The email is not added to the page address or browser history. Opening either sign-in page while already signed in sends you to Shop for a customer-only account, Today for a staff account, or **No brewery yet** if your account has no membership.
Pending invitations take priority: either sign-in page opens **Invitations** first when consent is waiting.

<Screen name="Portal forgot password" />

A used, expired, or wrong-audience invitation opens **Invite expired**. Sign in if you already joined, or select **Reset password**. Contact brewery staff if your customer access is missing. The brewery can remove your access to a customer. From your next page or action, even if you are signed in, MGR no longer shows or accepts that customer's orders; the orders and invoices you placed stay with the customer. Entry cards sit near the bottom of a phone screen and center on wider screens.

<Screen name="Account invitations" />

If you already have an MGR sign-in and the brewery invites that email to a customer account, signing in sends you to **Invitations** first; you can also open it yourself at `/invitations`. The page reminds you that joining grants access to the customer account shown and does not change your password. Each pending invitation names the brewery and the customer account offered; select **Accept invitation** to join. Access starts only after you consent, and accepting opens that customer account. Consent expires seven days after the invitation is sent; an expired or revoked invitation needs a new one from the brewery. An old accepted invitation cannot restore removed access; ask for a new one. If the brewery closes its MGR account, your sign-in for that brewery ends; ask the brewery for copies of invoices you need before it closes. With nothing pending, the page says **No pending invitations. Ask the brewery for a new invitation if yours expired.** If acceptance fails, MGR asks you to check you're signed in with the invited email and ask the brewery whether it expired or was revoked. If you belong to several customer accounts, open **Me**, choose **Customer account**, and select **Switch account**.

<Screen name="Portal set password" />

**Change password** in **Me** asks for **Current password**, a new password of at least eight characters that differs from your current one, and **Confirm new password**. Invalid values, mismatches, and an incorrect current password show beside the fields. Use **Show password** to check for typos and **Hide password** to mask it again. **Back to MGR** leaves without saving and returns to the page you opened from Me. From a direct visit or recovery link, **Back to MGR** returns a customer-only login to Shop. An unknown page offers **Back to Today**.

Forgotten your password, or sign in without one? Select **Need a reset link?** A verified reset link lets you choose and confirm a password without the old one, then sign in again on the portal sign-in page. Saving revokes other sessions' refresh access; issued access tokens can remain valid until expiry. If ending sessions fails after saving, **Password saved** stays visible even if you have been signed out. If you are still signed in, select **Sign out all sessions** to end the session before signing in again. Otherwise, sign in with the new password and use **Sign out** to end all sessions. You do not need to change the password again.

The header shows your customer account name. Navigation offers **Order**, **Orders**, **Invoices**, and **Account** as tabs along the bottom on a phone and a left rail on wider screens. **Order** is the shop. Open **Me** in the header to see the email you signed in with and account you represent, open **Guide** for this portal guide, **Change password**, or select **Sign out**. Me is a centered dialog at every screen size. It opens with keyboard focus on its **Me** title, so a stray Enter does not sign you out. Closing a form or dialog returns keyboard focus to the button that opened it. On every page, the first Tab stop is **Skip to main content**, which moves past the navigation to the page. Phone form panels sit above the bottom navigation; forms with pinned footer actions keep those actions visible while you scroll the fields. On a phone, actions such as **Pay invoice** span the column. On a wider screen they hug their label. If MGR says the credentials are incorrect, contact the brewery. A login that is not on any brewery or customer account shows **No brewery yet**; contact the brewery about access, then select **Sign out**. A staff login that is not on a customer account and opens a portal page shows **Portal is for customers** instead, with **Back to Today** and **Sign out**.

Screen readers can jump to **Primary** navigation for the left rail or the banner for the header controls. **Skip to main content** moves keyboard focus directly into the scrollable page, past both the navigation and header controls.

**Guide** opens in a new tab so your current portal page and entries remain in the original tab. Close the guide tab to return to that place, or select **Back to MGR** in the guide header to open the app.

Scroll within the page to reach lower items and actions. The header and phone tabs stay outside that scrolling area.

In an open drop-down list, use the arrow keys to move and Enter to choose. A tinted fill and a solid outline mark the active option in both light and dark themes.

## Shop [#shop]

<Screen name="Shop" />

*You see beers and the packages the brewery has designated for wholesale and priced for your account's sale channel: ½ keg, ⅙ keg, case, or bottle. Every price you see is the one your account's sale channel carries for that beer's price group and package format. A package that is not designated, or has no price for your account, is not on Shop. Warehouse counts are not shown. The brewery also has to have configured the warehouse that fulfills portal orders.*

The brewery's public taproom menu is a separate read-only website feed. Publishing a poured format there does not add it to this wholesale Shop, expose warehouse counts, or change your account's catalog and prices.

1. Review each beer, its listed packages, and the unit price.
2. Enter positive whole-unit quantities for the packages you want, using the minus/plus buttons or typing a number. Invalid quantities are rejected when saving or reviewing; a rejected save leaves the existing draft unchanged.
3. Select **Change** beside the ship-to summary to open **Delivery details**, then choose **Ship to**. Your default address is selected for a new cart.
4. Optionally enter **Requested date**, **PO number** and **Note** in Delivery details, select **Done**, then check the **Current catalog subtotal**. Clearing the requested date removes it from a saved draft. Shop states that tax and keg deposits are not in that subtotal, and that the brewery confirms the final invoice amounts and the requested delivery date. The date you enter is a request, not a commitment. A picked date reads like **Sep 10, 2026**.
5. Select **Review order**. MGR takes a current server quote for the selected ship-to, fulfillment source, channel prices, and returnable-keg deposits. Review shows the deposit separately. When the brewery has entitled QuickBooks tax calculation configured, it also shows calculated **Tax** and **Estimated total**. Otherwise it says **Tax pending**: the subtotal and deposits are current, and the invoice supplies the final tax and total. Select **Place order** (the amount is marked **before tax** when tax is pending), or choose **Back to edit**. Changing a quantity in Review returns you to Shop and requires a fresh review before placing the order. **Save draft** on Shop saves without submitting. The brewery reviews submitted orders before they are confirmed.

If QuickBooks needs to renew its authorization during review, MGR renews it and retries tax once automatically. If tax calculation remains unavailable, you can still place the order with **Tax pending**; the invoice supplies the final tax.

<Screen name="Review order" />

New carts select your default ship-to, or the first address if none is designated. You can choose another address; saving the cart keeps your explicit choice. **Select a ship-to** is a prompt, not an address you can choose. Contact brewery staff to change the account default.

**Unsaved cart:** this browser keeps a new cart's quantities and delivery details while you move around the portal, reload, or come back later. It is kept for your login and this customer account only; another login or account on the same browser starts its own cart. It is cleared when you select **Save draft** or place the order, and dropped after 7 days without an edit. If a package left the catalog since then, Shop removes it and names it in a message; a ship-to that no longer exists returns to your default. Drafts, reorders, and a pending order request are not part of this cart. Nothing is sent to the brewery until you save or place the order. If the browser blocks storage, the cart lasts only until you leave Shop; use **Save draft** to keep it.

Below the list, **Coming up** opens what the brewery plans to brew next: one row per beer and the week it is expected, from this week on, soonest first. A cancelled plan or a plan whose week has passed without brewing drops off. Rescheduling moves it to the new planned week. Tap a beer to jump to it on Shop. A beer marked **not yet listed** has no wholesale package to order yet; ask the brewery. Dates are the brewery’s plan and can move. Nothing else about a batch is shown.

<Screen name="Coming up" />

Save draft and Review order stay disabled without a ship-to, without a positive line, without a configured shipping source, or while saving. A message names what is missing: choose a ship-to, enter a quantity above zero, or both. Shop and review show **Ships from** with the brewery’s configured warehouse name. That holds even after a draft has been saved from this cart. If a pending request contains a package no longer available in the catalog, both the subtotal and Review order label say the amount is unavailable instead of showing a partial amount. With no priced products, Shop asks you to call the brewery.

Submitting rechecks the price, ship-to address, shipping source, keg deposit, and accounting company against the reviewed quote. If any changed, MGR creates no order and asks you to return to the cart and review the current details. A quote from a replaced accounting company stays as stale history and cannot be submitted with the new company's connection. After an uncertain submit response, **Retry order request** uses that exact reviewed quote and request; a later catalog change or quote expiry cannot create a duplicate or change a committed order.

For returnable kegs, the submitted order keeps the reviewed deposit price with the keg line. A saved draft submitted without a reviewed quote is charged, for each keg line, the keg pool's current deposit at the moment it is submitted. If brewery staff later adjusts the order, a retained keg line keeps that frozen price and a newly added keg line uses the deposit configured at adjustment time. A configured zero deposit remains a valid no-charge keg. The invoice charges each resulting frozen deposit price for the quantity actually shipped. A one-way keg, which you keep, has no deposit; **Review order** lists a **One-way keg fee** for each one-way keg line and counts it in the amount before tax, and the invoice charges that fee for each one-way keg you received. That fee is not refunded, even when the beer is returned.

<Callout type="warn">
**Before ordering:** if **Ships from** says **Not configured**, contact the brewery. Fresh saves and submissions stay disabled until the brewery configures its shipping warehouse. You can still retry an earlier uncertain request to confirm its outcome.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">
**Saved drafts and retries:** open a draft in Orders and choose **Continue / edit** to change or submit that same order. A submitted order can no longer be edited. After an uncertain response, fields stay locked and **Retry order request** reuses the exact request, including after reloading or signing back in on the same browser tab. No request is sent automatically. If another tab signs in to a different account, Retry refuses to send the order under that account; sign back in as the original buyer. Use **View order status** to check progress. Keep this tab open until the outcome is clear; closing it clears its recovery information. If recovery storage is unavailable, MGR does not start a new write. Contact the brewery if the outcome remains unclear.
</Callout>

From a shipped order, **Reorder** starts a new cart with still-active, currently priced packages and the previous quantities. Removed or unpriced packages are listed explicitly, by name, including packages the brewery has since retired. Prices come from the current catalog, not the previous invoice. A still-valid ship-to is kept; the old PO, note and requested date are not copied. Review the new cart before submitting.

## Order statuses [#statuses]

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `draft` | Saved, not sent to the brewery for fulfillment. |
| `submitted` | Sent to the brewery, waiting for review. |
| `confirmed` | Accepted by the brewery. Inventory is reserved. |
| `picked` | The warehouse recorded quantities prepared for shipment. If the brewery adds a line or raises a quantity after picking, the order returns to `confirmed` until the new quantities are picked. |
| `shipped` | The brewery recorded shipment. An invoice for positive shipped quantities is created at shipping, or, when the brewery chose invoicing on delivery, at delivery confirmation; a fully refused delivery creates none. |
| `cancelled` | The brewery stopped the order. The reason appears in the timeline. |

## Orders [#orders]

When the brewery confirms your order, MGR queues an email to each buyer linked to your customer account. Email delivery depends on the brewery's sending setup and may be delayed or blocked. Check the order here for its current status; an email reflects the details when it was confirmed.

Order history shows up to 50 records, newest first. **More** opens older records when available; **Newest** returns to the latest page.

<Screen name="Order history" />
<Screen name="Order detail" />

**Orders** lists your newest orders first with status and requested date. Select an order number to see ship-to, PO, note, each line's ordered and shipped quantities and amount, and the linked invoice.

If the brewery changed the order after you placed it, the order shows **The brewery adjusted this order. Quantities above are what ships.** It does not show the old quantities or the reason. A short shipment shows ordered and shipped quantities, the shortage reason the brewery recorded at picking or shipping, the final invoice amount, and explains that the unshipped quantity was cancelled when the order closed, so no units remain to ship. The linked invoice separately shows whether its balance is unpaid, paid, settled, or written off. Order details remain available from every history row. Drafts also offer **Continue / edit**, and shipped orders offer **Reorder**, including short shipments; these actions open the cart. Submitted orders cannot be edited or cancelled in the portal. With no orders, the page says **No orders yet**.

## Invoices and credits [#invoices]

Invoice history uses the same **More** and **Newest** controls to reach older invoices and credits.

<Screen name="Invoice history" />
<Screen name="Pay invoice" />

**Paid** is a payment status, not a settlement date. MGR does not supply an actual payment date for one or multiple payments. A partial cash payment remains due until the balance reaches zero; credit-only closure is **Settled**, not **Paid**. MGR does not display a settlement date.

Invoice details remain available after payment, and you can still ask the brewery a question.
MGR does not offer an invoice PDF download.

Status words remain visible independently of color. **Paid** and **Settled** use a success indicator. **Unpaid** and **Review** need attention and use a warning indicator; Unpaid alone does not mean overdue. **Voided**, **Deleted**, **Written off**, and **Credit** are neutral, not payment confirmations. Review means QuickBooks changes need review before online payment is available.

**Invoices** lists invoice or credit-memo number, kind, total, and current payment state. An invoice's due date is frozen at issue time from your account's terms: Due on receipt, Net 15, or Net 30. Later term changes do not move that date; credit memos have no inferred due date. Previously undated invoices use the current account terms because historical terms were not recorded. A credit memo is a separate negative billing record created by the brewery for a correction, a return, or a keg deposit refund. With no billing records, the page says **No invoices yet**. Open a number to see the record: total, issue date, invoice due date when applicable, its status, and each line's quantity and amount. After QuickBooks sync, the list, detail, and linked order show the current QuickBooks total; the original line amounts remain visible as billing history and can differ after an accountant edit. An invoice changed by an accountant says **Review** and does not offer Pay until the brewery reviews the change. An invoice that QuickBooks reports as voided, deleted, or written off says **This invoice is not payable**; it does not show a due date or ask you to pay, regardless of any earlier paid status. A live zero-balance invoice with no positive cash payment says **Settled** and is not payable; this can happen when a credit covers the balance. A live invoice with a current positive QuickBooks balance offers **Pay invoice** and opens the payment handoff in a new tab. MGR first checks that the invoice still belongs to your account, asks QuickBooks for a fresh payment link, then checks the balance and your access again before redirecting. A not-yet-known balance leaves payment unavailable. A reversed payment can reopen an invoice: the next sync updates its paid status, and its current positive balance makes it payable again. MGR stops checking a paid invoice 90 days after it first sees it paid, so a later reversal does not show here. MGR never saves or displays the QuickBooks payment link. If the invoice, connection, or link is unavailable, the payment page tells you to contact the brewery instead. A number that is not yours shows **Not found**.

Only choosing **Pay invoice** starts the payment handoff. Viewing the invoice,
following a saved page URL, or a browser prefetch does not spend the handoff
request budget or refresh the QuickBooks connection.


Opening a payment handoff shares your signed-in account's request limit with other portal activity. If too many requests arrive within a minute, **Payment unavailable** appears without contacting QuickBooks. Wait a minute, reopen the invoice, and try **Pay invoice** once. This refusal does not submit a payment. If it is still unavailable, contact the brewery using the invoice question action; MGR does not show provider errors or payment-link secrets.

<Screen name="Question invoice" />

To ask about a line, a total, or a payment, open the invoice or credit memo, even after an invoice is paid, and select **Ask about this invoice**, write your note (up to 2,000 characters; a counter under the box shows how many you have used, and longer pasted text is cut off at the limit), and select **Send to** the named brewery. The sheet confirms that it was sent; select **Close** to return to the invoice. The brewery's sales team sees it on their Today list and answers by phone or email; nothing on the invoice changes.

## Account [#account]

Your portal access covers your linked customer accounts and their priced catalog.
Internal accounting identifiers, tax classifications, licenses, payment terms,
and the brewery's full price grid are not available to buyer logins.

<Screen name="Account" />

**Account** shows the customer name on this login, each ship-to with its city and state and a default label when designated, the line **You · buyer** for this login, and any keg deposits the brewery holds for you; a keg deposit refund credit memo lowers that amount, and a voided or written-off invoice no longer counts toward it. Other people on the account are never listed. Open **Me** to sign out. There is no portal control to add ship-tos or change the account name; contact the brewery.

## Errors and current limits [#help]

Screen readers can read the informational and warning hints as notes without interrupting. Errors still announce as alerts.

**An action reports an error.** Read the message and fix the visible fields. Your entries stay on Shop until the action succeeds or you leave the page. If the error is about prices, ship-tos, access, or fulfillment setup, contact the brewery.

If the signed-in buyer account changes while an order or invoice question is pending, MGR refuses the action before writing. Return to the original account to retry an uncertain action unchanged, or check the saved order or question before starting again.

**A page fails to load.** Select **Try again** once. If it still fails, give the brewery your customer name, page, approximate time, and the action you tried. When a **Reference** appears under the error, include it.

**A page says "Not found".** A mistyped or unknown portal address, or a link to an order or invoice that does not exist for your account, shows Not found inside portal navigation with **Back to Orders**. The browser tab reads **Not found · MGR**, including a Shop link to a missing draft or reorder source. Check the address, or open **Orders** or **Invoices** and pick the record from the list.

<Screen name="Portal page not found" />

**A product, order, or invoice seems missing.** Confirm the customer account name shown in navigation. Shop includes only SKUs (a brand in one package format) the brewery has designated for wholesale and priced for that account's sale channel. Orders and Invoices include only that account's records.

There are currently no portal controls for public sign-up, adding ship-tos, changing account information, cancelling orders, or recording payment. A saved draft stays editable from **Orders** with **Continue / edit** until you submit it. **Account** is the tab for the customer name on this login.

If the application and this guide disagree, report the page, action, and exact message to your brewery contact.

## Screen walkthrough [#walkthrough]

In the [screen explorer](/docs/screens-explore), sign in and password recovery lead to Shop. The buyer is Jordan Lee throughout. Product rows stay in the portal. Orders and Invoices open their customer detail screens, and Reorder returns to Shop. Account addresses are read-only. Me offers Change password. Pay invoice stands in for a QuickBooks payment link; the walkthrough does not open a payment session.

If an invoice question loses its response, use **Retry saved request** in **Unresolved requests**. It sends the saved input with its original identity. Another question waits until the saved one is retried or discarded. If you have confirmed with the brewery that the question arrived, or that it did not, select **Discard saved request**, then **Confirm discard**. Nothing is sent, and you can ask again. Reloading the same browser tab preserves it; changing accounts hides it until you return to the original account. Nothing is sent automatically. Keep the tab open until the outcome is recovered.

If a payment link cannot open, MGR returns you to the invoice, says online payment isn’t available, and keeps **Ask about this invoice** available; contact the brewery to arrange payment.
