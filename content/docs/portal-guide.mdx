Your login starts with an invitation from the brewery. Open the email, enter **Your name**, choose a password of at least eight characters, and select **Join**. Your customer membership sends you to Shop. All invited customer users have buyer access; there is no Account admin role or public sign-up.

## Sign in and navigate [#access]

<Screen name="Portal sign in" />

Enter your **Email** and the **Password** you chose, then select **Sign in**. A customer-only account opens **Shop**. **Forgot password?** opens **Reset password**: enter your email and select **Send reset link**; the page says a link is on its way whenever that email is on an account, and the link opens **Set new password**.

A used, expired, or wrong-audience invitation opens **Invite expired**. Sign in if you already joined, or select **Reset password**. Contact brewery staff if your customer access is missing. The brewery cannot resend an invitation or attach an existing sign-in account through the invite form.

<Screen name="Portal set password" />

The header shows your customer account name. Navigation offers **Order**, **Orders**, **Invoices**, and **Account** as tabs along the bottom on a phone and a left rail on wider screens. **Order** is the shop. Open **Me** in the header to see the email you signed in with, **Switch to light mode** or **Switch to dark mode**, **Change password**, or select **Sign out**. On a phone, actions such as **Pay invoice** span the column. On a wider screen they hug their label. If MGR says the credentials are incorrect, or the account has no membership, contact the brewery.

## Shop [#shop]

<Screen name="Shop" />

*You see beers and the packages the brewery has designated for wholesale and priced for your account's sale channel — ½ keg, ⅙ keg, case, or bottle. Every price you see is the one your account's sale channel carries for that beer's price group and package format. A package that is not designated, or has no price for your account, is not on Shop. Warehouse counts are not shown. The brewery also has to have configured the warehouse that fulfills portal orders.*

1. Review each beer, its listed packages, and the unit price.
2. Enter positive whole-unit quantities for the packages you want.
3. Choose **Ship to**. The first address is selected by default.
4. Optionally enter **PO number** and **Note**, then check the **Subtotal**.
5. Select **Submit order** to save the order and send it to the brewery, or **Save draft** to save it without submitting. The brewery reviews submitted orders before they are confirmed.

New carts select your default ship-to, or the first address if none is designated. You can choose another address; saving the cart keeps your explicit choice. Contact brewery staff to change the account default.

Both buttons stay disabled without a ship-to, without a positive line, or while saving. That holds even after a draft has been saved from this cart. With no priced products, Shop says **No products available yet.**

<Callout type="warn">
**Before ordering:** if the brewery has not configured its portal fulfillment warehouse, saving fails with **portal fulfillment source is not configured**. Ask brewery staff to set that up.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">
**Draft limitation:** after **Save draft**, MGR opens a read-only order page. You cannot edit or submit that saved draft from the order page. Ask brewery staff to finish or cancel it. If you stay on Shop after saving (or after a submission MGR could not confirm), further edits to quantities, ship-to, PO number, or note replace the saved draft's contents the next time you select **Save draft** or **Submit order**. The same order is reused, so you do not get a duplicate draft. If MGR cannot confirm submission, use **View order status** before retrying: the order may already be submitted. Contact the brewery if the status is still unclear.
</Callout>

## Order statuses [#statuses]

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `draft` | Saved, not sent to the brewery for fulfillment. |
| `submitted` | Sent to the brewery, waiting for review. |
| `confirmed` | Accepted by the brewery. Inventory is reserved. |
| `picked` | The warehouse recorded quantities prepared for shipment. |
| `shipped` | The brewery recorded shipment and created an invoice for positive shipped quantities. |
| `cancelled` | The brewery stopped the order. The reason appears in the timeline. |

**staged — needs restocking** means the brewery changed an order after it had been picked, and warehouse staff still need to put the staged stock back.

## Orders [#orders]

<Screen name="Order history" />
<Screen name="Order detail" />

**Orders** lists your newest orders first with status, requested date, and any restocking badge. Select an order number to see ship-to, PO, note, ordered/picked/shipped quantities, captured unit prices, and the event timeline.

Brewery line adjustments show the quantities before and after, plus the reason. Cancellation reasons show up too. The page is read-only: you cannot edit, submit, or cancel an order from it. With no orders, the page says **No orders yet.** A new draft with no events says **No events yet.**

## Invoices and credits [#invoices]

<Screen name="Pay invoice" />

**Invoices** lists invoice or credit-memo number, kind, total, and paid date. A credit memo is a separate negative billing record created by the brewery for a correction or return. With no billing records, the page says **No invoices yet**. Open a number to see the record: total, issue and due dates, paid or unpaid status, and each line's quantity and amount. An unpaid invoice tells you to contact the brewery to pay; there is no online payment yet. A number that is not yours shows **Not found**.

<Screen name="Question invoice" />

The portal has no payment action. To ask about a line, a total, or a payment, open the invoice and select **Ask about this invoice**, write your note, and **Send**. The brewery's sales team sees it on their Today list and answers by phone or email; nothing on the invoice changes.

## Account [#account]

<Screen name="Account" />

**Account** shows the customer name on this login, each ship-to with its city and state and a default label when designated, the line **You · buyer** for this login, and any keg deposits the brewery holds for you. Other people on the account are never listed. Open **Me** to sign out. There is no portal control to add ship-tos or change the account name; contact the brewery.

## Errors and current limits [#help]

**An action reports an error.** Read the message and fix the visible fields. Your entries stay on Shop until the action succeeds or you leave the page. If the error is about prices, ship-tos, access, or fulfillment setup, contact the brewery.

**A page fails to load.** Select **Try again** once. If it still fails, give the brewery your customer name, page, approximate time, and the action you tried.

**A page says "Not found".** The order or invoice at that address does not exist for your account, or the link is wrong. Open **Orders** or **Invoices** and pick the record from the list.

**A product, order, or invoice seems missing.** Confirm the customer account name shown in navigation. Shop includes only SKUs (a brand in one package format) the brewery has designated for wholesale and priced for that account's sale channel. Orders and Invoices include only that account's records.

There are currently no portal controls for public sign-up, adding ship-tos, changing account information, editing saved drafts or submitting them after leaving Shop, cancelling orders, or recording payment. **Account** is the tab for the customer name on this login.

If the application and this guide disagree, report the page, action, and exact message to your brewery contact.

## Screen walkthrough [#walkthrough]

In the [screen explorer](/docs/screens-explore), sign in and password recovery lead to Shop. The buyer is Jordan Lee throughout. Product rows stay in the portal. Orders and Invoices open their customer detail screens, and Reorder returns to Shop. Account addresses are read-only. Me offers Change password. Pay invoice stands in for a QuickBooks payment link; the walkthrough does not open a payment session.
