Your login starts with an invitation from the brewery. Open the email, enter **Your name**, choose a password of at least eight characters, and select **Join**. Your customer membership sends you to Shop. All invited customer users have buyer access; there is no Account admin role or public sign-up.

## Sign in and navigate [#access]

<Screen name="Portal sign in" />

Enter your **Email** and the **Password** you chose, then select **Sign in**. A customer-only account opens **Shop**. **Forgot password?** opens **Reset password**: enter your email and select **Send reset link**; the page says a link is on its way whenever that email is on an account, and the link opens **Set new password**.

A used, expired, or wrong-audience invitation opens **Invite expired**. Sign in if you already joined, or select **Reset password**. Contact brewery staff if your customer access is missing. The brewery cannot resend an invitation or attach an existing sign-in account through the invite form.

<Screen name="Portal set password" />

The header shows your customer account name. Navigation offers **Order**, **Orders**, **Invoices**, and **Account** as tabs along the bottom on a phone and a left rail on wider screens. **Order** is the shop. Open **Me** in the header to see the email you signed in with, **Switch to light mode** or **Switch to dark mode**, **Change password**, or select **Sign out**. On a phone, actions such as **Pay invoice** span the column. On a wider screen they hug their label. If MGR says the credentials are incorrect, or the account has no membership, contact the brewery.

## Shop [#shop]

<Screen name="Shop" />

*You see beers and the packages the brewery has designated for wholesale and priced for your account's sale channel — ½ keg, ⅙ keg, case, or bottle. Every price you see is the one your account's sale channel carries for that beer's price group and package format. A package that is not designated, or has no price for your account, is not on Shop. Warehouse counts are not shown. The brewery also has to have configured the warehouse that fulfills portal orders.*

1. Review each beer, its listed packages, and the unit price.
2. Enter positive whole-unit quantities for the packages you want.
3. Choose **Ship to**. Your default address is selected for a new cart.
4. Optionally enter **Requested date**, **PO number** and **Note**, then check the **Current catalog subtotal**. Clearing the requested date removes it from a saved draft.
5. Select **Review order**. MGR takes a current server quote for the selected ship-to, fulfillment source, channel prices, and returnable-keg deposits. Review shows the deposit separately. When the brewery has entitled QuickBooks tax calculation configured, it also shows **Estimated tax** and **Estimated total**. Otherwise it says **Tax pending**: the subtotal and deposits are current, and the invoice supplies the final tax and total. Select **Submit order**, choose **Back to edit** to keep editing, or select **Save draft** without submitting. The brewery reviews submitted orders before they are confirmed.

New carts select your default ship-to, or the first address if none is designated. You can choose another address; saving the cart keeps your explicit choice. Contact brewery staff to change the account default.

Save draft and Review order stay disabled without a ship-to, without a positive line, without a configured shipping source, or while saving. Shop and review show **Ships from** with the brewery’s configured warehouse name. That holds even after a draft has been saved from this cart. With no priced products, Shop asks you to call the brewery.

Submitting rechecks the price, ship-to address, shipping source, and keg deposit against the reviewed quote. If any changed, MGR creates no order and asks you to return to the cart and review the current details. After an uncertain submit response, **Retry order request** uses that exact reviewed quote and request; a later catalog change or quote expiry cannot create a duplicate or change a committed order.

For returnable kegs, the submitted order keeps the reviewed deposit price with the keg line. If brewery staff later adjusts the order, a retained keg line keeps that frozen price and a newly added keg line uses the deposit configured at adjustment time. A configured zero deposit remains a valid no-charge keg. The invoice charges each resulting frozen deposit price for the quantity actually shipped.

<Callout type="warn">
**Before ordering:** if **Ships from** says **Not configured**, contact the brewery. Fresh saves and submissions stay disabled until the brewery configures its shipping warehouse. You can still retry an earlier uncertain request to confirm its outcome.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">
**Saved drafts and retries:** open a draft in Orders and choose **Continue/edit draft** to change or submit that same order. A submitted order can no longer be edited. After an uncertain response, fields stay locked and **Retry order request** reuses the exact request, including after reloading or signing back in on the same browser tab. No request is sent automatically. If another tab signs in to a different account, Retry refuses to send the order under that account; sign back in as the original buyer. Use **View order status** to check progress. Keep this tab open until the outcome is clear; closing it clears its recovery information. If recovery storage is unavailable, MGR does not start a new write. Contact the brewery if the outcome remains unclear.
</Callout>

From a shipped order, **Reorder** starts a new cart with still-active, currently priced packages and the previous quantities. Removed or unpriced packages are listed explicitly. Prices come from the current catalog, not the previous invoice. A still-valid ship-to is kept; the old PO, note and requested date are not copied. Review the new cart before submitting.

## Order statuses [#statuses]

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `draft` | Saved, not sent to the brewery for fulfillment. |
| `submitted` | Sent to the brewery, waiting for review. |
| `confirmed` | Accepted by the brewery. Inventory is reserved. |
| `picked` | The warehouse recorded quantities prepared for shipment. |
| `shipped` | The brewery recorded shipment and created an invoice for positive shipped quantities. |
| `cancelled` | The brewery stopped the order. The reason appears in the timeline. |

**staged — needs restocking** means the brewery changed an order after it had been picked, and warehouse staff still need to put the staged stock back.

## Orders [#orders]

<Screen name="Order history" />
<Screen name="Order detail" />

**Orders** lists your newest orders first with status, requested date, and any restocking badge. Select an order number to see ship-to, PO, note, ordered/picked/shipped quantities, captured unit prices, and the event timeline.

Brewery line adjustments show the quantities before and after, plus the reason. Cancellation reasons show up too. Order details remain available from every history row. Drafts also offer **Continue / edit**, and shipped orders offer **Reorder**, including short shipments; these actions open the cart. Submitted orders cannot be edited or cancelled in the portal. With no orders, the page says **No orders yet.** A new draft with no events says **No events yet.**

## Invoices and credits [#invoices]

<Screen name="Pay invoice" />

**Invoices** lists invoice or credit-memo number, kind, total, and current payment state. A credit memo is a separate negative billing record created by the brewery for a correction or return. With no billing records, the page says **No invoices yet**. Open a number to see the record: total, issue date, invoice due date when applicable, its status, and each line's quantity and amount. After QuickBooks sync, the list, detail, and linked order show the current QuickBooks total; the original line amounts remain visible as billing history and can differ after an accountant edit. An invoice that QuickBooks reports as voided, deleted, or written off says **This invoice is not payable**; it does not show a due date or ask you to pay, even when the brewery retains an earlier paid date in its records. When online payment is offered, MGR checks that the live invoice still belongs to your account and has a current positive QuickBooks balance, asks QuickBooks for a fresh payment link, checks the balance and your access again, and then sends you to QuickBooks. A zero or not-yet-known balance leaves payment unavailable. A reversed payment can reopen an invoice: its earlier paid date remains in the history, while its current positive balance makes it payable again. MGR never saves the payment link. If the invoice, connection, or link is unavailable, the payment page tells you to contact the brewery instead. A number that is not yours shows **Not found**.

<Screen name="Question invoice" />

To ask about a line, a total, or a payment, open the invoice or credit memo—even after an invoice is paid—and select **Ask about this invoice**, write your note, and **Send**. The brewery's sales team sees it on their Today list and answers by phone or email; nothing on the invoice changes.

## Account [#account]

<Screen name="Account" />

**Account** shows the customer name on this login, each ship-to with its city and state and a default label when designated, the line **You · buyer** for this login, and any keg deposits the brewery holds for you. Other people on the account are never listed. Open **Me** to sign out. There is no portal control to add ship-tos or change the account name; contact the brewery.

## Errors and current limits [#help]

**An action reports an error.** Read the message and fix the visible fields. Your entries stay on Shop until the action succeeds or you leave the page. If the error is about prices, ship-tos, access, or fulfillment setup, contact the brewery.

If the signed-in buyer account changes while an order or invoice question is pending, MGR refuses the action before writing. Return to the original account to retry an uncertain action unchanged, or check the saved order or question before starting again.

**A page fails to load.** Select **Try again** once. If it still fails, give the brewery your customer name, page, approximate time, and the action you tried.

**A page says "Not found".** The order or invoice at that address does not exist for your account, or the link is wrong. Open **Orders** or **Invoices** and pick the record from the list.

**A product, order, or invoice seems missing.** Confirm the customer account name shown in navigation. Shop includes only SKUs (a brand in one package format) the brewery has designated for wholesale and priced for that account's sale channel. Orders and Invoices include only that account's records.

There are currently no portal controls for public sign-up, adding ship-tos, changing account information, editing saved drafts or submitting them after leaving Shop, cancelling orders, or recording payment. **Account** is the tab for the customer name on this login.

If the application and this guide disagree, report the page, action, and exact message to your brewery contact.

## Screen walkthrough [#walkthrough]

In the [screen explorer](/docs/screens-explore), sign in and password recovery lead to Shop. The buyer is Jordan Lee throughout. Product rows stay in the portal. Orders and Invoices open their customer detail screens, and Reorder returns to Shop. Account addresses are read-only. Me offers Change password. Pay invoice stands in for a QuickBooks payment link; the walkthrough does not open a payment session.
