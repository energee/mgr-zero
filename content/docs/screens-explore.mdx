Find a screen by name, or by a word in what it does, its spec, or its states. Narrow by area or surface, then open it in the real shell.

Taps inside the screen walk to the next one: a row opens its detail, a verb opens the sheet it names, and Work tabs on the individual lists open their sibling lists. On the Work landing, chips filter the rows in place. Other tabs that name a screen open it; remaining tabs filter their rows. The rail switches areas.

A verb with nowhere to go still acts. An add-a-line button adds an unsaved line. A commit verb closes its sheet or returns along the trail, flashing done when there is nowhere to return.

Reset, page-error recovery, delivery outcomes, refused-beer check-in, standing-allocation release, and connected Accounting payment switches use local fixture state. Reset submission keeps its information on the same card; Back returns to the matching staff or portal sign-in. Try again replaces the error with its shared destination view. Delivery records the entered accepted and refused quantities, and check-in reduces the remaining refusal. Release opens the same confirmation used by the app: cancel leaves the reservation alone; confirmation removes it without moving stock or changing par. The release record starts on the replenishment page with that dialog open. These demonstrations make no Auth, command, email, or provider request; reopening a frame starts its fixture again.

Finished goods includes synthetic recent movement rows in the same register and history drawing as the app. Finished goods empty shows the same owner with no stock or movement history. These frames do not offer fabricated history-page links; the live page supplies its real pagination destinations.

The trail above the frame shows the walk. Its Back button retraces it, same as the browser's back button.

The persona menu signs you in as a demo person for each role. The header and Me sheet show them. The rail and the More entries filter to their role. Today becomes their landing. A screen their role may not open lands on No access, including the one in view when you switch.

The Phone toggle shows the selected screen and persona at phone width. Local controls still work there, but its taps do not follow the explorer's walk.

Row actions in these frames carry a soft color fill: purple for general work such as Edit and Finish, green for Confirm, orange for corrective work such as Put back and Assign, cyan for operational work such as Pick, Count, and Resume, and pink for destructive workflows such as Disconnect. These are the light-theme colors. In dark mode, corrective work is magenta. General work is blue text and destructive work is red text, with no fill, so the text stays readable on every surface. Selecting a row action opens the workflow. It does not itself commit the change.

Every link on this page reopens the same screen, persona, and filters. Under each frame, **In the inventory** jumps to the same screen on the [screen inventory](/docs/screens) page, and **Frame alone** opens it by itself.
