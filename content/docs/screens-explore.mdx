Find a screen by name, or by a word in what it does, its spec or its states,
narrow by area or surface, and open it in the real shell. Taps inside the
screen walk to the next one: a row opens its detail, a verb opens the sheet it
names, the Work chips open their own lists, a tab that names a screen opens
it and any other tab filters the rows under it, and the rail switches areas.
A verb with nowhere to go still acts: an add-a-line button adds an unsaved
line, and a commit verb closes its sheet or returns along the trail, flashing
done when there is nowhere to return. The trail above the frame shows the walk and
its Back button retraces it, as the browser's back button does. The persona
menu signs you in as a demo person for each role: the header and Me sheet show
them, the rail and the More entries filter to their role, Today becomes their
landing, and a screen their role may not open lands on No access, including
the one on view when you switch. The Phone toggle shows the selected screen at
phone width; that preview draws the default person and its taps do not walk.
Every link on this page reopens the same screen, persona and filters. Under
each frame, "In the inventory" jumps to the same screen on the
[screen inventory](/docs/screens) page and "Frame alone" opens it by itself.
