Start here

Sign in and navigate

Your account is created by invitation; there is no public sign-up or password-reset control. Enter the invited email address and password, then select Sign in. Customer-only accounts open Shop.

The navigation shows your customer account name and links to Shop, Orders, and Invoices. Select Sign out when finished. If MGR says the credentials are incorrect or the account has no membership, contact the brewery.

Place an order

Shop

You see only active SKUs priced on your assigned price list. The brewery must also have configured the warehouse used to fulfill portal orders.

  1. Review each product, package, unit price, and in stock, low stock, or out of stock badge. These badges are coarse; exact inventory is not shown.
  2. Enter positive whole-unit quantities. Out-of-stock items are disabled.
  3. Choose Ship to; the first address is selected by default.
  4. Optionally enter PO number and Note, then review the subtotal.
  5. Select Submit order to save the order and send it to the brewery, or Save draft to save it without submitting.

Both buttons stay disabled without a ship-to, without a positive line, or while saving. With no priced products, Shop says No products available yet.

Before ordering: if the brewery has not configured its portal fulfillment warehouse, saving fails with portal fulfillment source is not configured. Ask brewery staff to configure it.

Draft limitation: after Save draft, MGR opens a read-only order page. You cannot edit or submit that saved draft from the current portal. Ask brewery staff to finish or cancel it. If submission fails after a draft was saved, use View draft order to confirm what MGR kept, then contact the brewery.

What happens next

Order statuses

StatusMeaning
draftSaved but not sent to the brewery for fulfillment.
submittedSent to the brewery and waiting for review.
confirmedAccepted by the brewery; inventory is reserved.
pickedThe warehouse recorded quantities prepared for shipment.
shippedThe brewery recorded shipment and created an invoice for positive shipped quantities.
cancelledThe brewery stopped the order; its reason appears in the timeline.

staged — needs restocking means the brewery changed an order after it had been picked and warehouse staff still need to reconcile the staged stock.

Follow fulfillment

Orders

Orders lists your newest orders first with status, requested date, and any restocking badge. Select an order number to see ship-to, PO, note, ordered/picked/shipped quantities, captured unit prices, and the event timeline.

Brewery line adjustments show the quantities before and after and the reason. Cancellation reasons also appear. The page is read-only: portal users cannot edit, submit, or cancel an order from it. With no orders, the page says No orders yet; a new draft with no events says No events yet.

Billing records

Invoices and credits

Invoices lists invoice or credit-memo number, kind, total, and paid date. A credit memo is a separate negative billing record created by the brewery for a correction or return. With no billing records, the page says No invoices yet.

The portal currently has no invoice-detail or payment action. Contact the brewery for line-level invoice questions or payment corrections.

Recover safely

Errors and current limits

An action reports an error
Read the message and correct the visible fields. Your entries remain on Shop until the action succeeds or you leave the page. If the error concerns prices, ship-tos, access, or fulfillment configuration, contact the brewery.
A page fails to load
Select Try again once. If it still fails, give the brewery your customer name, page, approximate time, and action attempted.
A product, order, or invoice seems missing
Confirm the customer account name shown in navigation. Shop includes only active products priced for that account; Orders and Invoices include only that account’s records.

There are currently no portal controls for public sign-up, password reset, adding ship-tos, changing account information, editing or submitting saved drafts, cancelling orders, opening invoice detail, or recording payment.

If the application and this guide disagree, report the page, action, and exact message to your brewery contact.
