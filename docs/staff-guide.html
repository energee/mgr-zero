Current application behavior

Start here

Sign in and sign out

Staff accounts are created by invitation; there is no public sign-up or password-reset control.

  1. Enter the email address and password associated with your invitation.
  2. Select Sign in.
  3. MGR opens Dashboard for the active brewery.

If the credentials are wrong, MGR shows Incorrect email or password. If the account has no brewery membership, ask an administrator to assign access. The current staff navigation has no sign-out control.

Orientation

Navigate MGR

The brewery name at the top of the header identifies the brewery whose records are displayed. Navigation is four sections — Today, Beer, Work, and More — shown as tabs along the bottom on a phone and as a left rail on wider screens. Beer holds Inventory; Work holds Orders, Pick, and Replenishment; More holds Invoices, Catalog, Customers, Price lists, and Settings (the team roster). Links your role cannot use are simply not shown. The Me control in the header shows your brewery and role, switches between light and dark mode, and offers Sign out. Forms open as a panel from the bottom of the screen on a phone and as a centered dialog on wider screens.

DashboardA landing page only; it currently has no metrics or actions.
FormsA successful form closes and refreshes the page. An error stays in the open form with the entered values.
Closing a formClosing without saving clears changes and restores the form defaults.

Sellable beer

Catalog: products and SKUs

A product is the beer, such as Hazy IPA. A SKU is one sellable package of that beer, such as a half-barrel keg or a case of cans. Admin and Sales can create both; Warehouse can view them.

Create a product

  1. Open Catalog and select New Product.
  2. Enter Name (required), Style (optional), and ABV (optional percentage; enter 6.5 for 6.5%).
  3. Select Create. Product names must be unique within the brewery. The product appears alphabetically and initially shows No SKUs yet.

Create a SKU

  1. Find the product and select New SKU.
  2. Enter Name; choose Package type (keg, can, or bottle; default keg).
  3. Enter optional whole-number Units per case and required BBL per unit.
  4. Select Create. A SKU name must be unique within its product. The SKU becomes available in inventory, pricing, and order forms.

BBL per unit is the barrel volume of one inventory unit. One US beer barrel is 31 gallons (3,968 fl oz). Examples: half-barrel keg = 0.5; sixth-barrel keg ≈ 0.16666667; 24 × 16 oz case = 384 ÷ 3968 ≈ 0.09677419. Verify this value before recording inventory because the current Catalog has no edit control.

If there are no products, Catalog shows No products yet.

Finished goods

Inventory and movement history

Admin, Sales, and Warehouse can view inventory. Admin and Warehouse can record movements.

On handThe sum of recorded movements for one SKU at one location.
ATPAvailable to promise: total on hand minus open reservations for that SKU. It can be negative.
Movement logThe 50 newest entries: time, type, SKU, location, signed quantity, and note.

Record a movement

  1. Open Inventory and select Record Movement.
  2. Choose a SKU and Location.
  3. Choose a Type. The default is opening_balance.
  4. For depletion, leave Channel as taproom. Other displayed channel choices are rejected for this movement type.
  5. Enter Qty in SKU units, to two decimal places if needed. Use positive quantities for additions and negative quantities for removals; zero is rejected.
  6. Add an optional Note, then select Record.

Samples and festival removals: these require a destination state, but the current form has no destination-state field. Do not substitute another type; use your brewery’s approved recording procedure.

MGR calculates and stores barrel volume for every movement from quantity × the SKU’s BBL per unit. Movements cannot be edited or deleted. See Errors & corrections before correcting inventory.

Empty sections show No inventory recorded yet or No movements recorded yet.

Accounts and destinations

Customers and ship-tos

Admin and Sales can create and edit customers and ship-to addresses. The list shows name, type, home state, assigned price list, and payment terms.

Create or edit a customer

  1. Open Customers. Select New Customer, or select Edit beside an existing customer.
  2. Enter Name (required).
  3. Choose Type: distributor, retailer (default), brewery, or other.
  4. Enter the required two-letter State; MGR changes it to uppercase.
  5. Choose a Price list or None (default). Wholesale orders require an assigned list with a price for every ordered SKU.
  6. Optionally enter License number and Payment terms, such as net30.
  7. Select Create or Save.

Create or edit a ship-to

Select the customer name, then New Ship-To or Edit. Enter required Label, Address 1, City, State (two letters, changed to uppercase), and Zip; Address 2 is optional. Ship-to state determines the destination used when a wholesale shipment leaves inventory.

Empty lists show No customers yet or No ship-tos yet. Customer deletion and portal-user invitations are not available.

Customer prices

Price lists

Admin and Sales can create or rename lists and set a per-SKU price. A customer sees only the prices from its assigned list.

  1. Open Pricing and select New Price List. Enter a required name and select Create. The name must be unique within the brewery.
  2. Use Edit to rename a list; the new name must also be unique.
  3. Select Set Price, choose the SKU, and enter Unit price (USD) with up to cents. Zero is allowed.
  4. Select Save. Setting the same SKU again replaces its current price on that list.

A list without entries shows No prices set yet; an empty Pricing screen shows No price lists yet. There is no delete-price or delete-list control.

Sell, reserve, fulfill

Staff orders

Admin and Sales create and manage orders. Admin and Warehouse record picks and shipments. Admin, Sales, and Warehouse can view the order list and detail.

draftsubmittedconfirmedpickedshipped

An order may instead become cancelled any time before shipping. The Orders screen lists newest first and can filter by any status.

Create an order

  1. Open Orders and select New Order.
  2. Choose Kind: Wholesale (default) or Taproom transfer.
  3. For wholesale, choose the Customer and one of that customer’s Ship-tos. For a transfer, choose the destination taproom.
  4. Choose the source in From location. Use a warehouse source.
  5. Optionally choose Requested ship date and enter PO number.
  6. Add one or more lines. Each needs a SKU and quantity greater than zero. Add line adds a row; Remove removes one, but at least one row remains.
  7. Select Create. The result is a draft. Wholesale line prices are captured from the customer’s current price list; later price-list changes do not alter those lines.

A wholesale order fails if the customer has no price list or an ordered SKU has no price on that list.

The order detail shows customer and ship-to for wholesale orders, PO, requested date, ordered/picked/shipped quantity, captured price, current ATP, and a time-stamped event history. The current detail page does not display a taproom transfer’s destination. An empty order list says No orders yet; an empty timeline says No events yet. A shipped or cancelled order has no further action controls.

Warehouse queue

Pick sheet

Open Pick to see confirmed and picked orders grouped by requested ship date; orders without a date appear under Unscheduled. Each card shows ordered and previously picked amounts and links to the order detail, where picking is recorded.

Select Print to open the browser print dialog. MGR hides the staff navigation in the printed copy. If nothing is ready, the page says No confirmed orders to pick.

Billing record

Invoices and credit memos

Admin, Sales, and Warehouse can view invoices. Admin and Sales can create a credit memo.

The Invoices list shows number, kind, customer, total, and paid date. Open an invoice number to see issue date, line quantities, captured unit prices, amounts, and total. An empty list says No invoices yet. Payment is display-only; there is no record-payment action.

Correct a shipped invoice

  1. Open an invoice and select Credit memo.
  2. Enter the positive quantity to credit for each applicable line. Leave unrelated lines at zero. A credit cannot exceed the remaining uncredited quantity.
  3. Choose Return to location and enter a required Reason.
  4. Select Create.

MGR creates a separate credit memo at the original prices, adds the credited quantity back to the selected location, and records the correction on the originating order’s timeline. Credit memos cannot themselves be credited.

Taproom stock

Taproom replenishment

Admin and Sales can create replenishment orders. Warehouse can view the suggestions.

  1. Open Replenishment and choose a taproom. The first taproom is selected by default.
  2. Review each SKU’s par, on-hand amount, and suggested quantity. Suggested quantity is the par shortfall, never less than zero.
  3. Choose From warehouse; the first warehouse is selected by default when one exists.
  4. Edit Transfer qty as needed. Only positive lines are included.
  5. Select Create replenishment order. MGR creates it already confirmed, ready for warehouse picking.

The page says No taprooms configured when none exist, or No pars set for this taproom when there are no suggestions. The current interface has no controls to create locations or set par levels.

Staff roster

Team

Admin, Sales, and Warehouse can open Team to see current staff memberships. Each row shows a user ID and role; email addresses are not displayed. The page is read-only, so it has no invitation, role-change, or removal controls. An empty roster shows No staff members yet.

Private staff updates

Link your Slack account

When your brewery’s Slack connection is available, open its MGR App Home and choose Link MGR account. Sign in to MGR if asked. A valid link confirms that Slack is linked.

Linked App Home shows a role-filtered Today queue with up to 20 current items. Admin sees all live items; Sales sees submitted orders to review; Warehouse sees confirmed orders whose requested ship date is due. Select Open on an item to view its order in MGR. With no current work, App Home says You’re caught up.

Linked eligible staff also receive private Slack messages for newly submitted orders and picks that become due. The message contains a safe order label, requested-date detail, and Open in MGR; when the work resolves, the existing message changes to Resolved. MGR never posts customer contacts, prices, or notes to Slack.

If the link is missing, expired, already used, or belongs to another account, return to Slack and request a fresh link. Notification settings and unlink controls are not currently available in the MGR interface.

Recover safely

Errors and corrections

A form reports an error
Read the message, correct the visible fields, and submit again. Your entries remain until the form succeeds or you close it. Permission errors require an administrator.
A page fails to load
Select Try again once. If it still fails, give your administrator the brewery or customer name, page, approximate time, and action attempted.
A record seems missing
Confirm the brewery or customer account shown in the navigation, clear any order-status filter, and verify the exact product, SKU, customer, or location name.
Inventory is wrong
Do a physical count and identify what happened. Record an adjustment only for a verified count difference. Never disguise a sale, sample, loss, destruction, festival removal, return, or transfer as another type. Add a note that explains the correction.
A shipped order or invoice is wrong
Do not add an unrelated inventory movement. Create a credit memo from the invoice so the financial correction and returned stock stay linked to the shipment history.

Current limits

Actions not yet available

Do not look for hidden controls for these tasks:

  • public account sign-up or password reset;
  • switching breweries or signing out from the staff navigation;
  • staff or customer-portal invitations;
  • CSV import;
  • creating locations or setting taproom pars in the interface;
  • editing products or SKUs after creation;
  • deleting customers, ship-tos, prices, price lists, inventory history, orders, invoices, or credit memos;
  • editing staff roles or seeing staff email addresses;
  • recording payments;
  • Slack installation, notification settings, or unlinking from a visible MGR settings page.

Ask the person responsible for configuring your brewery when one of these tasks is required.

This manual describes controls that are available now. If the application and this guide disagree, report the screen, action, and exact message to your MGR administrator.
