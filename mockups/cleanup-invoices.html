Alternate state, not an additional active sync. Failures, superseded outcomes and recovery guidance remain expanded; optional history alone may collapse.

1 · Amounts keep their meaningInvoice total and last-read QuickBooks balance are different facts. Known zero is visible; missing stays unknown. Credits stay negative, never invented payable balances.
2 · Payment ≠ accountingDue, Paid and Settled are distinct from push failure, review, void and deletion. Date-only due days use the brewery’s local calendar; voiding is not payment.
3 · One list destinationOpen or Review leads to the invoice. A verified QuickBooks link remains secondary. Mobile reserves full-width facts above navigation, not beside three competing buttons.
