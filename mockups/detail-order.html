01 · State-aware amount, not a receivableThe main case is confirmed wholesale: $1,028 items + $120 deposits + $24 one-way fees = $1,172 before tax. Only one total per screen. Keg rows explain charges; they are not another grand total.
Draft variant: “Items estimate · $1,028.00”
“Keg deposits and fees not finalized until submission.” No all-in order amount and no $0.00 assumption for absent charges.
02 · The next valid task owns the headerConfirmed Admin / Warehouse: Record pick. Sales does not get a pick command. Submitted Admin / Sales: Review and confirm → /confirm, never a direct Confirm. Put back and changed-after-pick warnings sit immediately beside the workflow, not buried below items.
Transfer variant: “Taproom transfer · Not a customer charge.” No payable total / balance due. Picked Admin / Warehouse retains Complete transfer → /complete.
03 · No missing facts inventedZero ATP remains 0. Missing ATP is “Not supplied”; null picked / shipped is “—”. Source, ship-to, note, PO and long SKU names wrap. Container descriptors in this illustration require supplied facts before implementation; the existing detail snapshot does not identify container type.
Other states: Keep restock, re-pick, short reasons, compliance notes and empty / stale recovery states. Shipped cost and margin form a separate authorized section for Admin / Sales—not the price summary.

Design intent only. Shared system font and illustrative existing staff chrome; no new dashboard filters or status dropdowns. Phone panel intentionally includes all lines, charge details, history and secondary cancellation rather than clipping the page. Future implementation must bind the same OrderView and action presentation to inventory fixtures and authorized live adapters; these images do not establish that parity.
